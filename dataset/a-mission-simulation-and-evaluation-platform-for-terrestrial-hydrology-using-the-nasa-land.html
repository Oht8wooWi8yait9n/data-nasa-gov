A Mission Simulation and Evaluation Platform for Terrestrial Hydrology using the NASA Land Information System (LIS) Project

Earth ScienceDNG Legacy DataAccess: public
View Official Record on data.nasa.gov

Description

&lt;p&gt; Develop a mission simulation and evaluation platform for terrestrial hydrology missions by creating an end-to-end observation system simulation experiment (OSSE) platform by using NASA LIS&amp;rsquo; data assimilation, optimization, and uncertainty estimation tools.&amp;nbsp; This platform will enable:&lt;br /&gt; Quantifying the impact of observations through a variety of OSSE configurations and evaluation metrics related to terrestrial hydrologic science and applications&lt;br /&gt; Improving the use of products for end-use applications and science, and quantifying the mission risks for GPM and GRACE-II&lt;/p&gt;
