Description

ACE EPAM Electron Proton Alpha Monitor (EPAM) Level 2 intensities at 12-sec time resolution. Calibrated science quality data. The data are accessible via ftp in HDF and CDF from the ACE Science Center and CDAWeb, respectively, and in ASCII format from the value-added interfaces at those sites. The "parameter keys" given below are as used by CDAWeb.
