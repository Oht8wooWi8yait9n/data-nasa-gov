ACE Electron Proton Alpha Monitor (EPAM) Ephemeris Data, 5 min Averages

HeliophysicsScience Discovery EngineAccess: public
View Official Record on data.nasa.gov

Description

5min-averaged ephemeris data for the ACE spacecraft, Earth, and Jupiter. Also included are the orientation of the ACE spin-axis, and a rotation matrix for transforming from HEE cartisian coordinates to RTN coordinates.
