ACE Electron Proton Alpha Monitor (EPAM) LEFS150 MFSA, Solar Wind Frame, Sectored Electron Fluxes, Daily Averages

HeliophysicsScience Discovery EngineAccess: public
View Official Record on data.nasa.gov

Description

Daily-averaged sectored electron fluxes from the MF Spectrum Analyzer of the ACE/EPAM LEFS150 instrument. All energies thresholds take into account the incident particle type, shielding, and inactive dead-layer of the solid state detector. All fluxes are background corrected and are in the solar wind rest frame.
