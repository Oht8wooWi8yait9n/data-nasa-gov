ACE Electron Proton Alpha Monitor (EPAM) LEMS120 MFSA, Spacecraft Frame, Sectored Z>1 Ion Fluxes, Hourly Averages

HeliophysicsScience Discovery EngineAccess: public
View Official Record on data.nasa.gov

Description

Hourly-averaged sectored Z>1 ion fluxes from the MF Spectrum Analyzer of the ACE/EPAM LEMS120 instrument. All energies thresholds take into account the incident particle type, shielding, and inactive dead-layer of the solid state detector. All fluxes are background corrected and are in the spacecraft rest frame.
