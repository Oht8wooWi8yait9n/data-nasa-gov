Advanced Space Power Systems (ASPS): Advanced Microelectromechanical (MEMs) Photovoltaic Systems (AMPS) Project

DNG Legacy DataAccess: public
View Official Record on data.nasa.gov

Description

&lt;p&gt;Develop new cell and blanket technologies and manufacturing processes that reduce overall array costs&lt;/p&gt;&lt;p&gt;&amp;bull;Cells: develop new cell technology comparable to SOA with reduced cost at various production levels&lt;/p&gt;&lt;p&gt;&amp;bull;Blankets: develop blanket technology compatible with the space environment at costs less than SOA&lt;/p&gt;&lt;p&gt;&amp;bull;Electrical Interconnects and Cell Protection: develop automated processes for stringing cells and providing protection from the space environment&lt;/p&gt;&lt;p&gt;&amp;bull;Lab-scale development of technologies with greater power density and cost reduction through multiple programs tested for reliability by thermal cycling and space plasma interactions&lt;/p&gt;&lt;p&gt;&amp;bull;Flight-sized cell/blanket demonstrators for integration in SOA solar array structures&lt;/p&gt;
