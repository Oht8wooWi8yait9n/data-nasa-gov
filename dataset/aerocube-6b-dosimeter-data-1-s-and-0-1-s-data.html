HeliophysicsScience Discovery EngineAccess: public
View Official Record on data.nasa.gov

Description

Each AeroCube-6 vehicle carries three dosimeters measuring electrons with energies from about 43 keV to about 830 keV and protons with energies ranging from 370 keV to 12 MeV. The dataset manager, Dr. Paul O Brien, can be reached at paul.obrien@aero.org. The data are described in AeroCube-6 Dosimeter Data README (v3.0), Aerospace Report No. TOR-2016-01155, The Aerospace Corporation, March 4, 2016, El Segundo, CA. Also see AeroCube-6 Dosimeter Equivalent Energy Thresholds and Flux Conversion Factors, Aerospace Report No. TOR-2017-02598, The Aerospace Corporation, July 1, 2019, El Segundo, CA.
