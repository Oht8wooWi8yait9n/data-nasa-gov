Description

This bundle contains fixed-width ASCII files contains fully processed, calibrated temperature data for the upper and lower gradient bridge thermal sensors on both probes of the Heat Flow Experiment (HFE) at the Apollo 15 landing site. The time span is 02 April through 30 June 1975. These data were derived from raw measurements extracted from NASA's original Apollo Lunar Surface Experiments Package (ALSEP) archive tapes, also known as ARCSAV tapes.
