APOLLO 15 ALSEP/SWS SOLAR WIND 28-SEC RESOLUTION TABLES V1.0

Planetary ScienceScience Discovery EngineAccess: public
View Official Record on data.nasa.gov

Description

This data set contains daily tables of time ordered, 28-second-resolution plasma parameters, mainly of the solar wind, as observed on the Moon at the Apollo 15 ALSEP site by the Apollo 15 Solar Wind Spectrometer from 31 July 1971 through 30 June 1972.
