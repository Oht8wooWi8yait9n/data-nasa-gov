Apollo 17 Challenge System - Seed Germination (Cabbage, Brussel Sprouts, Radish and Lettuce)

Biological and Physical SciencesScience Discovery EngineAccess: public
View Official Record on data.nasa.gov

Description

Analyses of effects of lunar materials on plants: This study was designed to determine if lunar material can cause disease in plants grown under controlled conditions and to assess the effects of lunar materials on the germination and subsequent development of seeds of species belonging to the Tracheophyta (vascular plants). Due to the historic nature of these experiments some metadata is absent or unclear in the available documentation. At OSDR we welcome contributions from our community. If you review the material associated with this dataset and have a suggestion for an improvement, correction or addition please let us know at arc-dl-osdr-data@mail.nasa.gov.
