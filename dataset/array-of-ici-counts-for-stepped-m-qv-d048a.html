Planetary ScienceScience Discovery EngineAccess: public

Description

Mass spectrometric measurements of ions in the coma of comet Giacobini-Zinner made during the encounter of the International Cometary Explorer (ICE) on 11 September 1985, when the spacecraft passed within 7800 km of the nucleus on its antisunward side.
