Description

Measurements taken in Hudson Bay on board the icebreaker C.C.G.S. Amundsen to gain knowledge on marine coastal ecosystems as part of the ArcticNet program in 2005 and 2010. ArcticNet is a Network of Centres of Excellence of Canada to study the impacts of climate change in the Canadian North.
