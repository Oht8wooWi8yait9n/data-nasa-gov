ASTEROID FAMILY IDENTIFICATIONS V1.0

Earth ScienceDNG Legacy DataAccess: public
View Official Record on data.nasa.gov

Description

This is a compilation of the family memberships of numbered asteroids in five different asteroid family analyses. These include the analyses of Zappala et al., Williams, Kozai, Carusi and Massaro, and Hirayama.
