Borehole temperatures from mountain permafrost monitoring, Mongolia, Version 1

Earth ScienceScience Discovery EngineAccess: public
View Official Record on data.nasa.gov

Description

Location and description of some geocryological boreholes in Mongolia. Data include latitude, longitude, location, depth of permafrost top and bottom, and mean annual soil temperature. These data are presented on the CAPS Version 1.0 CD-ROM, June 1998.
