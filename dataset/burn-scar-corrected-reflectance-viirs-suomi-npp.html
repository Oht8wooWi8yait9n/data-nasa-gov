Burn Scar - Corrected Reflectance (VIIRS / Suomi-NPP)

Earth ScienceDNG Legacy DataAccess: public
View Official Record on data.nasa.gov

Description

This visualization represents a "false color" band combination (M11-I2-I1 | Red-Green-Blue) of data collected by the VIIRS instrument on the Suomi-NPP satellite and is useful for distinguishing burn scars from naturally low vegetation or bare soil.
