Description

The CAMEX-4 Andros Lisland Rawinsonde and Radiosondes dataset was collected by using numerous rawinsondes (radiosondes), which were launched from Andros Island in support of CAMEX-4. These sondes provided atmospheric soundings of temperature, pressure, relative humidity, wind, and altitude.
