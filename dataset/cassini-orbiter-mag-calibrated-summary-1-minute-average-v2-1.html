Description

This data set contains calibrated 1 minute averaged Cassini magnetometer data, for the entire Cassini mission. These data are provided in Kronographic (KRTP, KSO, KSM) coordinates at Saturn, Geographic coordinates (GSE, GSM) for the Earth flyby, Jovigraphic coordinates for the Jupiter flyby, and RTN coordinates for the entire mission. These data were calibrated, converted to physical coordinates and averaged to 1 minute by the Cassini MAG team at Imperial College.
