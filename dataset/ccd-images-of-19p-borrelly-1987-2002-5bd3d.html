CCD IMAGES OF 19P/BORRELLY, 1987-2002

Planetary ScienceScience Discovery EngineAccess: public
View Official Record on data.nasa.gov

Description

This data set presents images and photometry of comet 19P/Borrelly 1 obtained by Karen Meech over 20 nights during the period 1987-08-21 to 2002-02-03. These data were not of sufficient quality to be reviewed or formally ingested.
