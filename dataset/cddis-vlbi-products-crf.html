Description

Celestial Reference Frame (CRF) product derived from analysis of Very Long Baseline Interferometry (VLBI) data. These products are the generated by analysis centers in support of the International VLBI Service for Geodesy and Astrometry (IVS) and combined by the IVS analysis coordinator to form the official IVS CRF product.
