Description

The Clementine Basemap Mosaic is a full-resolution (100 meters per pixel) global mosaic produced by the U.S. Geological Survey from Clementine EDR Data. The 750 nanometer filter imaging data acquired by the Ultraviolet/Visible Camera were used to create the single-band basemap mosaic.
