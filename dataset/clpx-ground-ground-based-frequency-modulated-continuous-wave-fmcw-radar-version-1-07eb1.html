CLPX-Ground: Ground-Based Frequency Modulated Continuous Wave (FMCW) Radar, Version 1

Earth ScienceScience Discovery EngineAccess: public
View Official Record on data.nasa.gov

Description

This data set is comprised of FMCW radar measurements, which are sensitive to electromagnetic discontinuities in the snowpack made during the 2002 and 2003 Cold Land Processes Field Experiment (CLPX).
