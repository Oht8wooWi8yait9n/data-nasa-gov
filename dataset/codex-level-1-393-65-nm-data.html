Description

CODEX Level 1 393.65 nm Science images, 4 polarization states (0,45,90,135), and total brightness. This dataset is a member of the Collection of CODEX Level-1 Science Image dataset. Per instructions from the CODEX team, reference to the entire Collection (including the DOI, see below) should be used when this and other member datasets in the Collection are needed for citation purposes: NASA Goddard Space Flight Center (2026) CODEX Level-1 Science images [Collection]. Solar Data Analysis Center. https://doi.org/10.48322/2j05-bs76. Access date (assigned on date of landing page access). Partner Institutes: Korea Astronomy and Space Science Institute (KASI); Italian National Institute for Astrophysics (INAF)
