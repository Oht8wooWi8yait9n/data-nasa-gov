COLLECTED STARDUST/NAVCAM SHAPE MODELS OF 81P/WILD 2, V2.0

Earth ScienceDNG Legacy DataAccess: public
View Official Record on data.nasa.gov

Description

Shape models of comet 81P/Wild 2, as derived from the Stardust Navcam images obtained around the time of closest approach to the comet. Includes both the basic tri-axial ellipsoid shape and the detailed plate model shape.
