Planetary ScienceScience Discovery EngineAccess: public

Description

Shape models of comet 81P/Wild 2, as derived from the Stardust Navcam images obtained around the time of closest approach to the comet. Includes both the basic tri-axial ellipsoid shape and the detailed plate model shape. Version 2.1 corrects a labelling error in one of the data files.
