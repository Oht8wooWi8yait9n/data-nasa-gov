CRRES PROTEL 1-min proton distribution functions

HeliophysicsScience Discovery EngineAccess: public
View Official Record on data.nasa.gov

Description

This data set holds 1-min resolution proton distributions (19 5-deg pitch angles vs. 24 energies) from the Proton Telescope (PROTEL) on the CRRES spacecraft. The energy range covered is 1-100 MeV. The data are in SGI(UNIX) binary format. Read software is contained within the companion documentation.
