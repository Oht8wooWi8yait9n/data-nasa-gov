Description

Framing Camera 2 is one of two identical units flying on Dawn spacecraft. This dataset includes the Reduced Data Record (RDR) version of all available images acquired during the Mars Flyby phase. As in the original Raw data sets, in addition to the imagery, anciliary information is stored within the image headers, as well as a detailed account of all the reference files used for the calibartion. Calibration files needed for further processing are archived as a separate data set.
