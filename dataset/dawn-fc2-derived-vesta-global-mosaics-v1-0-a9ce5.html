Description

Abstract : This data set includes various Vesta global mosaics derived from images acquired by the Framing Camera 2 (FC2) on the NASA Dawn spacecraft at the high and low altitude mapping orbits (HAMO and LAMO). Data are provided in cylindrical and polar stereographic projections. In addition to the mosaics, the flatfield and stray light used during the processing of the raw images are provided.
