Description

Abstract ======== Framing Camera 2 is one of two identical units flying on Dawn spacecraft. This dataset includes the Experiment Data Record (EDR) version of all available images acquired during the initial checkout and all cruise phases (Earth to Mars, Mars to Vesta and Vesta to Ceres). In addition to the imagery, anciliary information is stored within the image headers. Calibration files needed for further processing are archived as a separate data set.
