Description

The GRaND RDR are a time-ordered collection ofcorrected gamma ray and neutron counting data and calibrated pulseheight spectra acquired by GRaND during all phases of the Dawn mission.This data set is specific to Mars Gravity Assist (MGA), and includesdata acquired during Mars approach and flyby. The RDR is a calibrateddata set derived from the Experiment Data Records (EDR), consisting oftime-series counting rates from which elemental abundances can bedetermined. Ancillary ephemeris and pointing data needed for analysisand mapping of the time series data are included in the data set.
