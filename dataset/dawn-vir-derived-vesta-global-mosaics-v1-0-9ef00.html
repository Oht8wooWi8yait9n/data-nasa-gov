Description

This data set contains global mosaics of the Dawn VIR band centers and depths of pyroxene absorption bands, located at 0.9 (band I) and 1.9 microns (band II), from the Vesta encounter. The data set was acquired during the (High Altitude Mapping Orbit(HAMO), Low Altitude Mapping Orbit (LAMO), and HAMO-2) mapping phases between 2011-09-30 and 2012-07-24 .
