DE 2 250ms IDM Ion Drift Velocities

HeliophysicsScience Discovery EngineAccess: public
View Official Record on data.nasa.gov

Description

This data set contains cross-track ion drift velocities obtained at 250ms resolution from the DE 2 Ion Drift Meter (IDM). Data are available in ASCII from nssdcftp and in CDF format from CDAWeb (as well as CDAWeb plots and lists).
