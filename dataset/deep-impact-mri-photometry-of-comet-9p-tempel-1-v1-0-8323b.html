Planetary ScienceScience Discovery EngineAccess: public
View Official Record on data.nasa.gov

Description

This dataset contains photometric measurements of comet 9P/Tempel 1 from images taken with the Medium Resolution CCD Instrument during the approach phase of the Deep Impact mission (from 1 May 2005 to 4 July 2005). These data, based on circular apertures ranging from 5 to 30 pixels in diameter, were derived from both science and navigation images taken through clear filters.
