Early Detection of Overheating Polytetrafluoroethylene Insulated Wires Project

DNG Legacy DataAccess: public
View Official Record on data.nasa.gov

Description

&lt;p&gt;The objective of the proposed work is to develop a rapid time response diagnostic using a suite of instruments for the detection of heated&amp;nbsp;polytetrafluoroethylene (PTFE) insulated wires based on chemicals and particles generated at relatively low temperatures. &amp;nbsp;The anticipated result of the proposed effort is the development of a conceptual design for an early warning system that can specifically detect the overheating of&amp;nbsp;polytetrafluoroethylene (PTFE) insulated wires. This design can be utilized as a subsystem integrated into a fully capable fire detection system.&lt;/p&gt;
