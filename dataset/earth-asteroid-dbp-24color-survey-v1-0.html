Earth ScienceDNG Legacy DataAccess: public
View Official Record on data.nasa.gov

Description

This dataset is comprised of asteroid flux data measured in 26 filters using the McCord dual beam photometer. Of the 26 filters used for the full dataset, a maximum of 24 were used for any one measurement. The wavelength range covered is from 0.32 to 1.08 micrometers.
