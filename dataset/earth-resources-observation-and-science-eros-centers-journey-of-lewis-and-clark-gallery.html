DNG Legacy DataAccess: public

Description

The Earth Resources Observation and Science (EROS) Center manages the this gallery of Landsat-derived images of one of the most remarkable and productive scientific explorations in American history. The Corps of Discovery expedition crossed the territory of the newly acquired but uncharted Louisiana Purchase.
