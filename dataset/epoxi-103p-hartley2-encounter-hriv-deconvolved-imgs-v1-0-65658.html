Description

This dataset contains deconvolved High Resolution Visible CCD (HRIV) images of the nucleus of comet 103/P Hartley 2. Clear and color filter (350-950 nm) images, which were acquired within +/- one hour of closest approach on 04 November 2010 at 13:59:47 UTC during the EPOXI mission, have been restored to retrieve much of the resolution that was lost due to the defocus of the HRI telescope. Image scales range from 1.4 to 85.5 m/pixel.
