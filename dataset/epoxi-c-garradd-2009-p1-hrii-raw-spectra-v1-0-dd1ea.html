Description

This dataset contains raw, 1.05- to 4.8-micron spectral images of comet C/Garradd (2009 P1) acquired by the High Resolution Infrared Spectrometer on 26 March and 02-03 April 2012 during the Cruise 3 phase of the EPOXI mission.
