EPOXI C/GARRADD (2009 P1) - MRI CALIBRATED IMAGES V1.0

Planetary ScienceScience Discovery EngineAccess: public
View Official Record on data.nasa.gov

Description

This dataset contains calibrated clear-filter, C2, CN, OH and dust continuum images of comet C/Garradd (2009 P1) acquired by the Medium Resolution Visible CCD (MRI) from 20 February through 09 April 2012 during the Cruise 3 phase of the EPOXI mission.
