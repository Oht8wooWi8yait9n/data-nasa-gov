Earth ScienceDNG Legacy DataAccess: public

Description

This dataset contains calibrated clear-filter, CN, OH and dust continuum images of comet C/ISON (2012 S1) acquired by the Medium Resolution Visible CCD (MRI) from 17 January through 06 March 2013 during the Cruise 3 phase of the EPOXI mission.
