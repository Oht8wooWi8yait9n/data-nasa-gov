EPOXI C/ISON (2012 S1) - MRI RAW IMAGES V1.0

Planetary ScienceScience Discovery EngineAccess: public
View Official Record on data.nasa.gov

Description

This dataset contains raw clear-filter, CN, OH and dust continuum images of comet C/ISON (2012 S1) acquired by the Medium Resolution Visible CCD (MRI) from 17 January through 06 March 2013 during the Cruise 3 phase of the EPOXI mission.
