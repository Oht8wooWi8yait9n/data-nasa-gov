Description

This dataset contains the raw and smoothed (averaged) instrument thermal telemetry for the entire EPOXI mission, from 04 October 2007 through 06 February 2011. Measurements were collected by 59 thermal sensors located in the HRII, HRIV, and MRI instruments, on the instrument platform, and on the solar wings of the Deep Impact flyby spacecraft. Version 2.1 corrects a typo in a column description in 5 data labels.
