EPOXI INFLIGHT LUNAR CALS - HRII CALIBRATED SPECTRA V1.0

Planetary ScienceScience Discovery EngineAccess: public
View Official Record on data.nasa.gov

Description

This dataset contains calibrated, 1.05- to 4.8-micron spectral images of the Moon acquired the High Resolution Infrared Spectrometer (HRII) from 29 December 2007 through 18 December 2009 during several in-flight instrument calibrations for the EPOXI mission.
