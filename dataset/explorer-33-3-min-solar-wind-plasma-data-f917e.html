Explorer 33 3-min solar wind plasma data

HeliophysicsScience Discovery EngineAccess: public
View Official Record on data.nasa.gov

Description

This data set has 3-min resolution solar wind plasma parameters (flow speed and direction angles, ion thermal speed and density) from the MIT Faraday Cup on Explorer 33 (AIMP 1, IMP D). There are no spacecraft position data in the records. The data are in annual ASCII files.
