Description

&lt;p&gt; &lt;strong&gt;Methodology used to answer science question:&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/p&gt; &lt;p&gt; The investigators propose to upgrade the successfully flown EUNIS EUV spectrograph, increasing its signal-to-noise ratio and replacing its 30-37 nm channel with a 52.4-63.0 nm channel to cover transition region lines.&amp;nbsp; They plan to fly the upgraded suborbital experiment, and analyze the results from this and the two previous EUNIS flights.&lt;/p&gt;
