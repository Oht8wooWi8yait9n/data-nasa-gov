Fabrication and Modeling of Carbon and Boron Nitride Nanotube Growth on Lightweight Substrates for Multi-Functional Composite Structures Project

DNG Legacy DataAccess: public
View Official Record on data.nasa.gov

Description

&lt;p&gt;&amp;nbsp;Specific objectives are:&lt;/p&gt;&lt;ol&gt;&lt;li&gt;Develop the fabrication process for producing fuzzy fiber with desired&amp;nbsp;carbon nanotube (CNT) length leveraging current collaboration with MIT through the National Space Technology Fellowship (NSTRF).&lt;/li&gt;&lt;li&gt;Evaluate fuzzy fiber composites with tailored CNT length&lt;/li&gt;&lt;li&gt;Develop&amp;nbsp;Boron Nitride Nanotube (BNNT) capabilities to enable incorporation of vertically aligned BNNTs into composite structures for multifunctional composite structures.&lt;/li&gt;&lt;li&gt;Model nanotube length and loading requirements to maximize transport properties through the resin rich inter-ply region of a composite and verify the model from composite data with different CNT lengths.&lt;/li&gt;&lt;/ol&gt;
