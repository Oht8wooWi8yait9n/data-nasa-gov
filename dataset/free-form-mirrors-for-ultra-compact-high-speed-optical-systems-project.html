Free Form Mirrors for Ultra Compact High Speed Optical Systems Project

DNG Legacy DataAccess: public
View Official Record on data.nasa.gov

Description

&lt;p&gt;This task is to collaboratively design and fabricate the free-form WIRIS telescope primary mirror with our partner Zygo as a technology demonstration. Fabricating the primary is challenging as it has 865 um of aspheric departure and is not rotationally symmetric.&lt;/p&gt;
