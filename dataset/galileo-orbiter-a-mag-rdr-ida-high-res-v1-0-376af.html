Description

Galileo Orbiter Magnetometer (MAG) calibrated high-resolution data from the Ida flyby in spacecraft, EME-50, RTN and Ida Solar Ecliptic (IaSE) coordinates. These data cover the interval 1993-08-28 15:56 to 1993-08-28 17:50.
