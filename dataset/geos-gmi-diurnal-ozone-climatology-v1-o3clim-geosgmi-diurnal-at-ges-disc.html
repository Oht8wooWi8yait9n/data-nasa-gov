Description

The GEOS-GMI Diurnal Ozone Climatology product (O3CLIM_GEOSGMI_DIURNAL) contains ozone climatology by averaging 2 years of GEOS-GMI model simulations (2017–2018) as a function of latitude in 5 degree bins, pressure, month and time of day (local solar time). The data are stored in a single netCDF (classic old version) file. Data are reported every half-hour for each month and zonally averaged at 5 degree resolution, from 87.74 to 0.32 hPa at 40 increments. The single data file size is 3.2 MB.
