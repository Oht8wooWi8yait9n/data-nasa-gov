Description

The GOES-19 Advanced Baseline Imager (ABI) Level 3C Sea Surface Temperature (SST) dataset, produced by the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA), provides gridded observations of sea surface temperature derived from satellite-based thermal infrared measurements acquired by the ABI instrument aboard the GOES-19 geostationary satellite. Originally launched as GOES-U on 25 June 2024 and renamed GOES-19 after reaching geostationary orbit, the satellite operates at an altitude of approximately 35,800 km and continuously observes a Full Disk domain spanning approximately 15°W–135°W and 60°S–60°N. The ABI is a 16-band multispectral imager designed to provide enhanced SST retrieval capability through improved radiometric calibration, image navigation, co-registration accuracy, spectral fidelity, and advanced preprocessing. SST retrievals are derived from thermal infrared observations acquired every 10 minutes and provide near-continuous monitoring of ocean surface thermal conditions across the GOES Full Disk domain. The Level 3C SST product is generated by the NOAA Advanced Clear-Sky Processor for Ocean (ACSPO) system from collated Level 2 Preprocessed (L2P) SST retrievals. The Level 3C product provides hourly gridded SST fields on a regular 0.02-degree equal-angle grid and is distributed in netCDF4 format compliant with the GHRSST Data Specification Version 2 (GDS2). Compared with the source L2P product, the L3C product offers reduced data volume and a regularly gridded representation that simplifies visualization, analysis, and data assimilation workflows. The dataset supports applications including operational oceanography, weather forecasting, marine environmental monitoring, climate studies, ecosystem assessments, and satellite data assimilation.
