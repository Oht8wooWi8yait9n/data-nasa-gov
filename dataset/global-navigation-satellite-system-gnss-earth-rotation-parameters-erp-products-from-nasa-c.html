Description

Earth Rotation Parameters (ERPs) derived from analysis of Global Navigation Satellite System (GNSS) data. These products are the generated by analysis centers in support of the International GNSS Service (IGS) and combined by the IGS analysis coordinator to form the official IGS ERP product (weekly).
