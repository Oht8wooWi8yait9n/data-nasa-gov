Description

The UltraViolet Spectrometer (UVS) on the Galileo spacecraft took unique data of Comet Shoemaker-Levy/9's impact with Jupiter. A preliminary analysis of this data is presented in this submission to the Planetary Data System (PDS). It consists of four small tables with detached labels and documentation.
