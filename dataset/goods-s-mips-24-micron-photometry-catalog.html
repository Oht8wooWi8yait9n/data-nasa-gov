Description

The Great Observatories Origins Deep Survey (GOODS) aims to unite extremely deep observations from NASA's Great Observatories (Spitzer, Hubble and Chandra), ESA's Herschel and XMM-Newton, and the most powerful ground-based facilities. The aim is to survey the distant universe to the faintest flux limits across the broadest range of wavelengths. \n\nThis catalog provides a list of sources for the MIPS 24 micron imaging of the GOODS-S field. It is limited to flux densities greater than 80 microJy, where the source extraction is highly complete and reliable.
