Description

The GPM Ground Validation Campaign Reports OLYMPEX dataset consists of flight reports, weather forecasts, instrument reports, scientist summaries, and plan-of-day reports collected during the Global Precipitation Measurement (GPM) Olympic Mountains Experiment (OLYMPEX) field campaign to help support the ground validation of the GPM. These campaign reports were collected during the intense operating period which occurred during November 2015 to February 2016. The various campaign reports are available in PDF, JPG, PNG, and Microsoft Powerpoint and Word formats, some of which are located within tarred data files.
