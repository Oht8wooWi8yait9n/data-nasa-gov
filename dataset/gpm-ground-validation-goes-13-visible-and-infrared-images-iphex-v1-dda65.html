Description

The GPM Ground Validation GOES 13 Visible and Infrared Images IPHEx dataset contains visible and infrared images in 3 sizes (FULL, CONUS, and EXT) from the GOES 13 Imager obtained during the Integrated Precipitation and Hydrology Experiment (IPHEx) field campaign that took place in the southeast region of the United States. This collection of GOES 13 images are available at 30 minute (EXT and CONUS) and 3 hour (FULL) intervals for dates between May 1, 2014 and June 16, 2014. The GPM Ground Validation GOES 13 IPHEx data files are in PNG format.
