Description

The GPM Ground Validation Pluvio Precipitation Gauge University of Connecticut (UCONN) dataset consists of precipitation intensity and precipitation accumulation collected using the OTT Pluvio2 weighing rain gauge during the three consecutive winter seasons at the University of Connecticut's main campus. Data files in this dataset are available in ASCII-CSV format from November 30, 2021 through May 16, 2024
