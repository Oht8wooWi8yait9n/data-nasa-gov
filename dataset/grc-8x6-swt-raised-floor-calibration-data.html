Description

This is a database of relevant data from the Boundary Layer Ingesting Inlet / Distortion Tolerant Fan (BLI2DTF) Raised Floor Calibration Test in the 8-foot by 6-foot Supersonic Wind Tunnel at NASA Glenn Research Center completed in May 2016. This database accompanies the report from that test: Stefanie M. Hirt, David J. Arend, John D. Wolter, and Aaron M. Johnson. "Development of a Flow Field for Testing a Boundary-Layer-Ingesting Propulsor", NASA/TM-2017-219554 (https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20205009731) & AIAA 2017-5043 (https://doi.org/10.2514/6.2017-5043)
