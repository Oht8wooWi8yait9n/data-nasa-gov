Description

GOODS-Herschel (Elbaz et al, 2011) is in ESA open time key project consisting of the deepest Herschel observations of the two Great Observatories Origins Deep Survey (GOODS) fields in the Northern and Southern hemispheres.\n\nThe GOODS catalogs include photometry from Spitzer IRAC & MIPS and Herschel PACS & SPIRE observations.
