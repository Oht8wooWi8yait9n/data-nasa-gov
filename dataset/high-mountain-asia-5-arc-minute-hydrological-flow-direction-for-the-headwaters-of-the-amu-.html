Description

TEMP: This High Mountain Asia (HMA) data set consists of a 5 arc-minute hydrological flow network for parts of Afghanistan, Tajikistan, Kyrgyzstan, and Pakistan primarily the headwaters of the Amu Darya and Indus River basins.
