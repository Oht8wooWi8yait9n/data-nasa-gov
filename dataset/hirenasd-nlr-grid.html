HIRENASD NLR grid

DNG Legacy DataAccess: public
View Official Record on data.nasa.gov

Description

Structured multiblock grid of HIRENASD wing with medium grid density, about 10 mill grid points, 9.5 mill cells. Starting from coarse AIAA AEPW structured grid, heavily modified for: - Improved boundary-layer resolution at the wing-body junction. - Possibility of defining multigrid levels (4 levels). - Improved topology suitable for in-house computers.
