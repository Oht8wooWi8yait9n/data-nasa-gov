Description

This data volume contains 17 channel spectral image cubes of asteroid 243 Ida ranging from 0.7 to 5.2 micrometers in wavelength in cgs units of radiance. These data were obtained by the Galileo spacecraft Near Infrared Mapping Spectrometer on August 28, 1993. They were radiometrically calibrated using calibration measurements obtained by the Near Infrared Mapping Spectrometer during its observations of Europa on June 28, 1996.
