Planetary ScienceScience Discovery EngineAccess: public

Description

The Hubble Space Telescope Wide Field Planetary Camera 2 data archive contains calibrated data of Mars observed between April 27, 1999 and September 4, 2001. These are full-disk images of Mars obtained primarily through narrowband filters and in the wavelength interval of 255 to 1042 nm. The data have been calibrated to radiance factor, or I/F.
