Earth ScienceDNG Legacy DataAccess: public

Description

The DWE data set consists of the sky frequencies measured at the Green Bank and Parkes telescopes (data from other stations may be included when they become available; see [BIRDETAL2005]), the retrieved zonal wind speed along the descent path, geometrical parameters used to separate the Huygens velocity components and documentation.
