Description

This dataset contains Level 2 (L2) land surface temperature (LST) and emissivity from the Hyperspectral Thermal Emission Spectrometer (HyTES) instrument. This collection includes the standard emissivity product for HyTES channels from 8-11.5 micrometers, PC regression (PCemis) modeled emissivity from 7.4-12 microns with the same dimensions as the Level 1A radiance data, the LST derived from atmospherically corrected radiance, and in-scene atmospheric corrections (ISAC). This is the NASA Earth Observing System Data and Information System (EOSDIS) facility instrument archive of these data. The data are provided in HDF-5 format.
