IBEX High Energy Neutral Atom Imager (Hi) Data Release 08, Omnidirectional Five-Year Ribbon ENA Flux Maps, J2000 Ecliptic Coordinates, Level H3

HeliophysicsScience Discovery EngineAccess: public
View Official Record on data.nasa.gov

Description

IBEX-Hi Release 8 five-year Ribbon-component maps derived by separating Ribbon energetic neutral atom emissions from the globally distributed flux using the first five years of IBEX observations (2009-2013). This CDF contains omnidirectional differential hydrogen ENA flux maps for IBEX-Hi energy channels 2-6 on a 60 by 30 J2000 right ascension and declination grid. Channel 2-6 FWHM energy ranges are 0.52-0.95, 0.84-1.55, 1.36-2.50, 1.99-3.75, and 3.13-6.00 keV; the corresponding nominal energies are 0.71, 1.11, 1.74, 2.73, and 4.29 keV. The CDAWeb ProductKey is IBEX_H3_ENA_HI_R08_OMNI_F2-RIBBON-MAPS-J2000_5YR.
