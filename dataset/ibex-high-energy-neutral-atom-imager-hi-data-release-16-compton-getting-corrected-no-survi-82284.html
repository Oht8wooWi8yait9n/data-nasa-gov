IBEX High Energy Neutral Atom Imager (Hi) Data Release 16, Compton-Getting corrected, No Survival Probability correction, ANTIRAM mono-energy West Ecliptic ENA Maps, Level H3, 11-year Data

Description

IBEX-Hi Release 16 11-year antiram-directional hydrogen ENA maps with Compton-Getting correction applied and no survival-probability correction. The product provides a single 11-year average covering mission years 2009 through 2019 in Solar West Ecliptic coordinates. MONO denotes the mono-energy Compton-Getting-corrected map representation. The science arrays use a 60 by 30 longitude-latitude grid for IBEX-Hi energy channels 2-6. The channel 2-6 FWHM energy ranges are 0.52-0.95, 0.84-1.55, 1.36-2.50, 1.99-3.75, and 3.13-6.00 keV; the corresponding nominal energies are 0.71, 1.11, 1.74, 2.73, and 4.29 keV. The CDF includes a dimensionless fit-parameter map (Parm), differential flux, signal-to-noise ratio, and flux-variance maps, together with virtual _plasma alternate-view variables. The CDAWeb ProductKey is IBEX_H3_ENA_HI_R16_CG_NOSP_ANTIRAM_MONO_11YR.
