IBEX High Energy Neutral Atom Imager (Hi) Data Release 16, Compton-Getting corrected, Survival Probability corrected, ANTIRAM mono-energy West Ecliptic ENA Maps, Level H3, one-year Data

Description

IBEX-Hi Release 16 one-year antiram-directional mono-energy hydrogen ENA maps with Compton-Getting and survival-probability corrections applied. The product provides one-year maps for mission years 2009 through 2019 on a 60 by 30 Solar Ecliptic longitude-latitude grid. The survival-probability correction accounts for ENA losses during transport through the heliosphere and scales the data from the IBEX observing location near 1 AU to approximately 100 AU. The mono designation identifies mono-energy Compton-Getting-corrected maps. The channel 2-6 FWHM energy ranges are 0.52-0.95, 0.84-1.55, 1.36-2.50, 1.99-3.75, and 3.13-6.00 keV; the corresponding nominal energies are 0.71, 1.11, 1.74, 2.73, and 4.29 keV. The CDF contains a dimensionless fit-parameter map (Parm), differential flux, signal-to-noise ratio, and flux-variance maps, plus virtual _plasma alternate-view variables and Flux*_movie map-movie views. The CDAWeb ProductKey is IBEX_H3_ENA_HI_R16_CG_SP_ANTIRAM_MONO_1YR.
