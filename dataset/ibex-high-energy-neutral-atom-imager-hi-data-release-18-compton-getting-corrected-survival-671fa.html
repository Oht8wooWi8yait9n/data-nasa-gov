Description

IBEX-Hi Release 18 one-year RAM hydrogen ENA maps for the lower GDF separated-map product from the Release 18 Ribbon/GDF separation. Compton-Getting correction has been applied to account for spacecraft motion, and survival-probability correction has been applied to account for ENA losses during transport and to scale fluxes from the IBEX observing location near 1 AU to approximately 100 AU. The data cover 2009 through 2022, with measurement intervals spanning 2008-12-25 through 2022-12-26, on a 60 by 30 Solar Ecliptic longitude-latitude grid for IBEX-Hi energy channels 2-6. The channel 2-6 FWHM energy ranges are 0.52-0.95, 0.84-1.55, 1.36-2.50, 1.99-3.75, and 3.13-6.00 keV; the nominal energies are 0.71, 1.11, 1.74, 2.73, and 4.29 keV. The source metadata identifies this as the lower GDF separated-map variant. The CDF variables described by this record include coordinate/time support arrays and total exposure-time maps for channels 2-6, including virtual CDAWeb alternate views. The CDAWeb ProductKey is IBEX_H3_ENA_HI_R18_CG_SP_RAM_LOWER_GDF_1YR.
