Description

This data set contains bathymetry of Antarctic ice shelves based on measurements from IceBridge Sander AIRGrav L1B Geolocated Free Air Gravity Anomalies (IGGRV1B) and IceBridge LDEO Gravimeter Suite L1B Geolocated Free Air Gravity Anomalies (IGLGS1B). The data were collected as part of Operation IceBridge funded aircraft survey campaigns.
