Description

This data set presents images of the nucleus of comet 1P/Halley obtained by the various contributing observers of the International Halley Watch (IHW) Near-Nucleus Studies Network (NNSN) over the period 16 October 1982 to 12 April 1989. Observers around the world contributed to this data set, which consists of 3523 well-documented FITS images. The second version includes PDS format and standard updates, as well as correction of the various errata noted since the original publication.
