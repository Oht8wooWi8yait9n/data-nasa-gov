Planetary ScienceScience Discovery EngineAccess: public

Description

The data set consists of ground-based images of comet 19P/Borrelly in the R filter taken at the Kitt Peak 2.1m for three nights from September 21-23, 2001, bracketing the Deep Space 1 encounter. Raw, as well as reduced images are included.
