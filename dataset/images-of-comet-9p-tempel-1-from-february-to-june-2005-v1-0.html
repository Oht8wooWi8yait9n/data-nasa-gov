IMAGES OF COMET 9P/TEMPEL 1 FROM FEBRUARY TO JUNE 2005 V1.0

Earth ScienceDNG Legacy DataAccess: public
View Official Record on data.nasa.gov

Description

The data set consists of raw and reduced ground-based images of comet 9P/Tempel 1 in the broadband R and narrowband HB filters taken at the Kitt Peak 2.1m telescope from February to June 2005 prior to the Deep Impact encounter.
