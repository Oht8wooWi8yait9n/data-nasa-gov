Description

This set of Interball Auroral Probe 2-min key parameters contains spectra and anistropy of electrons within energy ranges of 20-40 keV from two time-of-flight detectors EM-1-1 and EM-1-2. The field of view of these detectors are directed oppositely and perpendicular to the satellite rotation axis. See more information at http://www.iki.rssi.ru/interball/
