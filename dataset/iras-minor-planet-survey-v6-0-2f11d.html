Description

The Supplemental IRAS Minor Planet Survey (SIMPS) presents additional and revised diameters and albedos for asteroids detected by the 1983 IRAS mission. SIMPS is considered to supersede the diameters and albedos of the original 1992 IRAS Minor Planet Survey (IMPS).
