Description

This data set contains spectral observations of 37 comets obtained with the Short-wavelength prime (SWP) spectrograph on the International Ultraviolet Explorer (IUE) satellite. Both low dispersion data from 1150-1975 A and high dispersion data from 1145-1930A (with partial coverage from 1930-2198A) are included.
