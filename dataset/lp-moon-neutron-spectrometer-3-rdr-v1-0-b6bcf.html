Description

Neutron Spectrometer data acquired by Lunar Prospector have been reduced to four maps, which can be interpreted in terms of elemental composition of the lunar regolith: thermal neutrons, epithermal neutrons, moderated neutrons, and fast neutrons. The maps are presented as 720x360 arrays equally-spaced in latitude and longitude.
