Description

MAVEN Solar Wind Electron Analyzer (SWEA) electron energy/angle (3D) distributions in units of differential energy flux (eV/cm^2 sec ster eV) at the MAVEN survey telemetry rate. Description of the SWEA instrument can be found on the SWEA home page https://lasp.colorado.edu/maven/science/instrument-package/swea/ and in the SWEA instrument publication https://doi.org/10.1007/s11214-015-0232-1.
