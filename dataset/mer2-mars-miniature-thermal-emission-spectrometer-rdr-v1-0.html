Description

This archive contains Mars Exploration Rover Miniature Thermal Emission Spectrometer (Mini-TES) Reduced Data Record (RDR) products and ancillary files. The Mini-TES RDR products archived on this volume are the original products used during mission operations by the Mars Exploration Rover project.
