MESSENGER gravity and topography spherical harmonics model bundle

Earth ScienceDNG Legacy DataAccess: public
View Official Record on data.nasa.gov

Description

The bundle contains derived models of Mercury gravity and topography from radio science tracking and from the MLA instrument on MESSENGER. There are three product types: the Occultation Summary (OCC), the Spherical Harmonics ASCII Data Records (SHADR), and the Spherical Harmonics Binary Data Records (SHBDR).
