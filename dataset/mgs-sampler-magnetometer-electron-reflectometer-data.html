Description

MAG/ER data included on this volume consist of a suite of GIF files thatare plots of derived electron and magnetic field observations acquired during the assessment orbits [see: Acuna et al., 1998]. The plots include electron fluxes at 10, 50, 130, 300, and 1000 eV, a color spectrogram of the same data, and magnetic field amplitude and rms, and spacecraft altitude. The numerical data will be released later on MAG/ER archive volumes, after further validation and calibration procedures have been applied.
