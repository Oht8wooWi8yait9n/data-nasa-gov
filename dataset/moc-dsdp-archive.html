Description

This data set contains portions of the MOC Decompressed Standard Data Product (DSDP) Archive, a collection of decompressed images from the Mars Orbiter Camera on the Mars Global Surveyor spacecraft. Images are stored with PDS labels, but are otherwise unprocessed and uncalibrated.
