MODIS Mosaic of Antarctica 2008-2009 (MOA2009) Image Map, Version 2

Earth ScienceScience Discovery EngineAccess: public
View Official Record on data.nasa.gov

Description

This data set includes two image maps - a snow grain size map and a surface morphology map - provided at two different resolutions of 125 m and 750 m. The maps were produced from composited image swath data acquired by the Moderate Resolution Imaging Spectroradiometer (MODIS) over Antarctica for the 2008-2009 austral summer season.
