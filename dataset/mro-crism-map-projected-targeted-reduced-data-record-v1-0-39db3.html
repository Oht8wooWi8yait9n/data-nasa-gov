Description

This volume contains the CRISM Map-projected Targeted Reduced Data Record (MTRDR) archive, a collection of multiband image cubes derived from targeted (gimbaled) observations of Mars' surface acquired by the Compact Reconnaissance Imaging Spectrometer for Mars (CRISM) instrument on the Mars Reconnaissance Orbiter (MRO) spacecraft. Post-processing attempts to represent the spectrum the instrument would have measured looking at the surface of Mars at a standard illumination geometry, in the absence of atmospheric gases, with aerosol scattering normalized to that at the geometry within the observation that is closest to nadir, in the absence of instrument artifacts. A series of value added products represent spatial variability in signatures of minerals of interest. The data are map projected using terrain models of the Martian surface. A companion Targeted Empirical Record (TER) archive contains mostly the same data in the sensor space of the IR detector to support map projection using future more accurate terrain models.
