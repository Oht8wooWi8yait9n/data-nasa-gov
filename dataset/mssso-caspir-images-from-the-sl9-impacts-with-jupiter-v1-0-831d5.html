Description

This archive contains images of the collision of Comet Shoemaker-Levy 9 with Jupiter obtained with CASPIR on the Australian National University 2.3 m telescope at Siding Spring Observatory, Australia, by Peter J. McGregor and Mark G. Allen. Each subdirectory contains data for one night. A more specific description of the state of the data is given below.
