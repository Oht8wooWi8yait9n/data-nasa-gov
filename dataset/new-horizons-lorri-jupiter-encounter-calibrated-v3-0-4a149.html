Description

This data set contains Calibrated data taken by the New Horizons Long Range Reconnaissance Imager instrument during the Jupiter encounter mission phase. This is VERSION 3.0 of this data set. LORRI science observations for the Jupiter Phase include observations of the Jovian system: the Jovian atmosphere and clouds including full disk rotation movie and resolved images of selected features like the Great Red Spot and the Little Red Spot; Jovian ring system and associated small satellites; the Galilean satellites with particular attention to Io under both sunlit and eclipse conditions, and eclipse observations of both Europa and Ganymede; the small irregular satellites Elara, Himalia, Callirrhoe. Additional observations were taken to test and/or characterize and/or calibrate the modes and performance of the LORRI. LORRI V3.0 provides a few updates from LORRI V2.0. The lossy images were recalibrated, including expanding the 'bad' pixel designation of 8x8 boxes affected by the first 34 pixels of header information in the calibrated quality map. Also, updates were made to the documentation and catalog files, primarily to resolve liens from the V2.0 peer review. No new observations were added with Version 3.0
