Description

['"Understanding physiologic reactions to weightlessness is an indispensable requirement for safe human space missions. While adaptations of human organ systems in response to weightlessness have been described in former studies, their molecular background needs further elucidation. The study aims to analyse changes in the expression of circulating miRNAs in serum in response to gravitational changes induced by parabolic flight as a spaceflight analogue."']
