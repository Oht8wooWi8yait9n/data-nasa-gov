Description

The NASA-ISRO Synthetic Aperture Radar (NISAR) Level 0B Radar Raw Signal Science Urgent Response collection contains NISAR Level 0B products with expedited processing. The granules are removed some time after the granules have been processed through the normal production pipeline and are available in the associated standard Level 0 product collections.
