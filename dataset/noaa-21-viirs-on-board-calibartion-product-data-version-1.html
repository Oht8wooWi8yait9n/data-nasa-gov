Description

The VIIRS Geolocation Onboard Calibrator (OBC)-IP file contains solar diffuser observations, the associated gain state and HAM side information, and all engineering and housekeeping data, including unscaled data from the Solar Diffuser Stability Monitor (SDSM)/VIIRS Earth View Radiometric Calibration Unit and the Solar Diffuser GEO angles.
