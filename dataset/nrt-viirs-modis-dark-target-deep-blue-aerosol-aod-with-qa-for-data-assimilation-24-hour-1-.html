Description

The VIIRS/MODIS L3 product is designed to facilitate both evaluation and NRT applications for the NASA Dark Target (DT) and Deep Blue (DB) aerosol products for both the VIIRS and MODIS instruments. Consistent L3 aggregation is applied to each product after filtering using pre-defined QA thresholds. The product is generated in NRT, and provides a single combined product for VIIRS (NOAA-20 and SNPP) and MODIS (Aqua and Terra) for both the DT and DB NASA AOD retrievals. This AERDA_D3_VIIRS_MODIS_NRT product contains 24 hours of data (UTC 00:00 to 00:00 each day). It is produced once daily after all upstream data is available. The separate AERDA_L3_VIIRS_MODIS_NRT product (differentiated by "L3" as opposed to "D3") is divided into 3-hour data files (centered at UTC 00:00, 03:00, and so on each day), and each of the 3-hour files is updated in NRT as upstream L2 data becomes available.
