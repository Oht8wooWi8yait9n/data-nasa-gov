Description

The ODY MARS GAMMA RAY SPECTROMETER 5 AND data set is a table of neutron data from the NS sub-system of the Mars Odyssey Gamma-Ray Spectrometer that have been averaged (AND) over 5-degree latitude by 5-degree longitude spatial grids and 15-degrees of aerocentric solar longitude (Ls). The AND are an intermediate data product. These data are calculated from the derived neutron data (DND) and result in maps of thermal,epithermal, and fast neutron counting rates.
