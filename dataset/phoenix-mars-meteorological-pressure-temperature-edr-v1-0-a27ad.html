Description

The PHX METEOROLOGICAL DATA product contains pre-processed (Digital Numbers) temperature and pressure data. The temperature data was collected at 250, 500 and 1000mm above the Phoenix Lander deck, and the pressure data was collected at (nearly) the height of the Lander deck. Nominally the data was collected at 2 sec resolution, but is also provided at 512 sec averages (with distribution statistics).
