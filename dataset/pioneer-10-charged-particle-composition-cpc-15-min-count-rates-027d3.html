Pioneer 10 Charged Particle Composition (CPC) 15-min Count Rates

HeliophysicsScience Discovery EngineAccess: public
View Official Record on data.nasa.gov

Description

This data set contains coincidence mode counts and accumulation times, plus "box counts" based on pulse height analysis (PHA), at 15-min resolution. There are 11 coincidence mode counts and 27 "box counts." Taken together, the parameters included allow computation of average counting rates and of fluxes of protons, electrons, helium and CNO nuclei in selected energy windows corresponding to the PHA boxes. There are 12 proton boxes spanning 3-67 MeV, 5 helium boxes spanning 3-67 MeV/n, 5 electron boxes, 2 Z>2 boxes, 1 box each for CNO, Z>5, and E>67 MeV/n.
