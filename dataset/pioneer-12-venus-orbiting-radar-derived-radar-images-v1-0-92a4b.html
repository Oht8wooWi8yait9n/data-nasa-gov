Description

This data set consists of five VICAR2 format images of Venus derived from Pioneer Venus radar altimeter measurements. Data are presented in simple cylindrical projection format. The radius image contains 1440 lines (latitude) and 2880 samples (longitude), and the others contain 1024 lines and 2048 samples.
