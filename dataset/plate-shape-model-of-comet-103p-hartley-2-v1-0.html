PLATE SHAPE MODEL OF COMET 103P/HARTLEY 2 V1.0

Earth ScienceDNG Legacy DataAccess: public
View Official Record on data.nasa.gov

Description

Shape model of comet 103P/Hartley 2, as derived from the Deep Impact spacecraft images obtained around the time of closest approach to the comet during the EPOXI mission. Includes maps of the surface features on the nucleus.
