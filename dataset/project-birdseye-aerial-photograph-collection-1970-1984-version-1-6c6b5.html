Project Birdseye Aerial Photograph Collection, 1970 - 1984, Version 1

Earth ScienceScience Discovery EngineAccess: public
View Official Record on data.nasa.gov

Description

This collection consists of both analog and digital aerial photographs from Arctic areas in and around Baffin Bay, the Labrador Sea, the Arctic Ocean, the Beaufort and Chukchi Seas, and the Bering Sea and Strait from 1962 to 1984 as well as data reports from 1962 through 1971.
