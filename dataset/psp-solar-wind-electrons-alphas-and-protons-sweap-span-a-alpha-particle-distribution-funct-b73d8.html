PSP Solar Wind Electrons Alphas and Protons (SWEAP) SPAN-A Alpha Particle Distribution Function, Partial Moments, Instrument Frame, Level 3 (L3), 14 s Data

HeliophysicsScience Discovery EngineAccess: public
View Official Record on data.nasa.gov

Description

Parker Spiral Probe SPAN-Ion instrument Partial moments of the Alpha distribution function in the instrument, spacecraft, and RTN coordinate systems. User should be aware that the full ion distribution is typically NOT in the FOV of the instrument. The cadence varies depending upon instrument mode typical values are 3.5 s, 7 s and 224 s.
