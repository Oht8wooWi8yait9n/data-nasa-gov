PSRPI Mapping the Galactic distribution of pulsars with the VLBA

Space ScienceDNG Legacy DataAccess: public
View Official Record on data.nasa.gov

Description

Observations for the initial PSRPI sample of 60 pulsars have now concluded. 84.7 hours were observed on the VLBA (plus 12 hours from a pilot project) searching for in-beam calibrators around 245 pulsars, and 690.2 hours were utilised for 285 astrometric observations.
