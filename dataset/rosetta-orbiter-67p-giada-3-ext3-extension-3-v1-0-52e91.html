Description

Rosetta Extension 3 Phase covers the period of time from 1 July 2016 until 30 September 2016. It started after Rosetta successfully completed the Extension 2 Phase. The present DataSet collects the GIADA data of EXT3 phase. The GIADA Scientific phase started on 7 May 2014 and ended on 30 September 2016. It was devoted to the characterization of the 67P environment.
