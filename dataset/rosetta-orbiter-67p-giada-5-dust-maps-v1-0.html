Description

Rosetta-GIADA scientific Phase covers the period of time from 01 August 2014 until 31 September 2016. It started after the first dust particle detection by the GIADA subsystems. The present DataSet collects all the High Level GIADA dust maps obtained by high level elaboration of the measurements obtained during the scientific phase. The high level data produced are dust maps of the coma reporting spatial flux and mass distribution, spatial speed distribution and dispersion of the particles end dust production area on the nucleus.
