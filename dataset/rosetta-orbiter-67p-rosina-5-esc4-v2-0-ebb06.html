Description

This data set contains CODMAC level 5 science data acquired by the DFMS and RTOF ROSINA sensors between 2015-03-11 and 2015-06-30 during the Escort phase 4 of the Rosetta mission at the comet 67P/CG. V2.0: Some minor species (CH4, NH3, HCN, H2CO, C2H6, CH3OH, H2S, C2H5OH, OCS, CS2) detected by DFMS were added.
