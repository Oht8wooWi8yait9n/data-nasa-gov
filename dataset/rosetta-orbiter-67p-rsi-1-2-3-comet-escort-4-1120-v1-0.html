Description

This is a Rosetta Radio Science data set, collected during the COMET ESCORT 4 phase 2015-10-22 to 2015-12-31. It is a Global Gravity measurement at the comet 67P and covers the time 2015-10-22T10:21:00.000 to 2015-10-22T17:21:22.500.
