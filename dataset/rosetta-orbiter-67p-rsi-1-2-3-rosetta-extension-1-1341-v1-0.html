Description

This is a Rosetta Radio Science data set, collected during the ROSETTA EXTENSION 1 phase 2016-01-01 to 2016-04-05. It is a Global Gravity measurement at the comet 67P and covers the time 2016-01-14T06:03:35.000 to 2016-01-14T09:15:30.000.
