Description

This CODMAC level 4 data set contains radiometric calibrated and geometric distortion corrected (resampled) image data in reflectance units (normalized and thus without unit), acquired by the OSIRIS Wide Angle Camera on the Rosetta spacecraft during the CRUISE 2 mission phase, covering the period from 2005-04-05T00:00:00.000 to 2006-07-28T23:59:59.000. The prime objective was commissioning / calibration. Several targets of opportunity were observed, but none of them is identifiable as prime target. This version V1.0 is the first version of this dataset.
