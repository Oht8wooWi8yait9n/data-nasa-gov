Description

This data set contains images acquired by the OSIRIS Narrow Angle Camera during the COMET ESCORT 4 phase of the Rosetta mission at the comet 67P, covering the period from 2015-10-20T23:25:00.000 to 2015-11-17T23:24:59.000.
