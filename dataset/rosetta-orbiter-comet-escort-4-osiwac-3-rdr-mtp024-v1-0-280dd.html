Description

This data set contains images acquired by the OSIRIS Wide Angle Camera during the COMET ESCORT 4 phase of the Rosetta mission at the comet 67P, covering the period from 2015-12-15T23:25:00.000 to 2016-01-12T23:24:59.000.
