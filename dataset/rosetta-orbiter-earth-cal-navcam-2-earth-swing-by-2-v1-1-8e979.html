Description

This dataset contains ROSETTA NAVCAM RAW DATA of the Cruise Phase EAR2 from 16th Sep 2007 to 22nd Jan 2008 during the 2nd Earth Fly By on its journey to comet 67P/CG. This data set V1.1 supersedes the V1.0. It includes updates of the Browse images, adding of the FITS version, clarification of calibration target, document updates and resolve other minor outstanding errata.
