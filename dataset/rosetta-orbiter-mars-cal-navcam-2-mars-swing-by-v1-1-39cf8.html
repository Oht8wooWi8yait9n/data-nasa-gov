Description

This dataset contains ROSETTA NAVCAM RAW DATA of the Cruise Phase from 27th Nov 2006 to 24th Feb 2007 during the Mars Fly By on its journey to comet 67P. This data set V1.1 supersedes the V1.0. It includes updates of the Browse images, adding of the FITS version, clarification of calibration target, document updates and resolve other minor outstanding errata.
