Description

The SeaWiFS Deep Blue Level 3 monthly product contains monthly global gridded (0.5 x 0.5 deg) Version 4 data derived from SeaWiFS Deep Blue Level 3 daily gridded data at the same spatial resolution. The primary data parameters are aerosol optical thickness, and Angstrom exponent.
