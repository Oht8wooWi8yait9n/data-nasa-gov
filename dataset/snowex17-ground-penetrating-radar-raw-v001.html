Description

This data set contains the RAW and UNPROCESSED results of a ground penetrating radar survey conducted as part of the 2017 SnowEx campaign. The processed data can be found here: https://dx.doi.org/10.5067/NPZYNEEUGQUO Data were collected between 08 February 2017 and 25 February 2017 at Grand Mesa, Colorado. Grand Mesa is a snow-covered, forested study site about 40 miles east of Grand Junction, Colorado.
