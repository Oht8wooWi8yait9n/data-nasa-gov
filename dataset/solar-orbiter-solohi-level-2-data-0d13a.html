Description

The Solar Orbiter Heliospheric Imager (SoloHI) calibrated data with bias, stray light and vignetting corrections applied. The images are rectified so that the right side of the image corresponds to spacecraft +X (sunward) direction. Data units are Mean Solar Brightness (MSB).
