Description

The Spitzer Atlas of Stellar Spectra presents IRS Short-Low and Long-Low spectra of 159 stars selected to provide a complete sampling of the HR diagram. \n\nThe SASS Catalog presents the spectral type, luminosity type, color, metallicity, and photometry for each star in the Atlas.
