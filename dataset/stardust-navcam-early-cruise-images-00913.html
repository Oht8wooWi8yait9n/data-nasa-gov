Description

This volume contains the results of the early cruise images of the Stardust Navigation Camera. These images are of no clear scientific or engineering use. They were acquired to ascertain the state of the camera during a time of successive contaminations and decontamination actions, as described in detail below. None are considered useful for calibration of future scientific images, given the nearly continuous changes in the state of the camera during this period.
