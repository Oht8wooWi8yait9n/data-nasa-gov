Description

This data set contains the results of the preflight calibration of the Stardust Navigation Camera. The images were collected in an attempt to mimic the environment that the camera would experience during cruise and encounter with the comet Wild-2. These data are saved for historical reasons. They are not considered to be of archival quality.
