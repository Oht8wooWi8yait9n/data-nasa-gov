STEREO-A In-Situ Measurements of Particles and CME Transients (IMPACT) Fluxgate Magnetometer (MAG) Burst Mode, Magnetic Field Vector, Radial-Tangential-Normal (RTN) Coordinates, Level 1 (L1), 31 ms Data

HeliophysicsScience Discovery EngineAccess: public
View Official Record on data.nasa.gov

Description

This data product contains Level 1 0.03125-s or 32 Hz burst-mode values of solar wind magnetic field data measured by the IMPACT Magnetometer on STEREO-A in Radial-Tangential-Normal, RTN, coordinates.
