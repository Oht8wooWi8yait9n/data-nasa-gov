STEREO-A multi-instrument Level 1 IMPACT data from UCB

HeliophysicsScience Discovery EngineAccess: public
View Official Record on data.nasa.gov

Description

Level 1 data from each of the several IMPACT instruments, in CDF. Time resolutions range from 0.125s (MAG) to 1m (SEPT, LET, HET). Full spectral and directional resolution is preserved in the data. Links are also given on the page to browse plots and to sites with data in ASCII format for some specific instruments.
