Description

This data set presents STEREO-A PLASTIC PickUp Ion, PUI, He⁺ phase space densities, PSD, in the spacecraft frame of reference. The various data sets provide 10 min, 1 hr, and 24 hr averages of He⁺, phase space density as a function of V/Vsw in 51 velocity ranges, where V and Vsw are the PUI particle speed and bulk speed of solar wind protons, respectively. These data are delivered to the public domain on a regular basis. Efforts are made to include the latest known calibration and background determinations, however, these are expected to undergo revision. We therefore suggest that users regularly return to this page, and make sure to get the most recent revision.
