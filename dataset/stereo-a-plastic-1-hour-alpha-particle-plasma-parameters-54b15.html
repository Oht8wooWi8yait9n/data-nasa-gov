STEREO-A PLASTIC 1-hour Alpha Particle Plasma Parameters

HeliophysicsScience Discovery EngineAccess: public
View Official Record on data.nasa.gov

Description

This data product contains 1-hour averaged values of solar wind alpha particle flow speed, density, thermal speed, alpha to proton density ratio, and proton-alpha flow speed difference.
