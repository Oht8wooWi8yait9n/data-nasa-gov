STEREO-B Plasma and Suprathermal Ion Composition (PLASTIC) 2-hour Iron Plasma Parameters

HeliophysicsScience Discovery EngineAccess: public
View Official Record on data.nasa.gov

Description

This data product contains 2-hour averaged values of solar wind iron flow speed, density, thermal speed, iron to proton density ratio, and quality flags for each of these parameters. It also contains the iron charge state used in the V and Vth (1D Maxwellian) fitting algorithm
