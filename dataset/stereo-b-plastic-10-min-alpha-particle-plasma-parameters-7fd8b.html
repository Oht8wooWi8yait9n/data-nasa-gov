STEREO-B PLASTIC 10-min Alpha Particle Plasma Parameters

HeliophysicsScience Discovery EngineAccess: public
View Official Record on data.nasa.gov

Description

This data product contains 10-min averaged values of solar wind alpha particle flow speed, density, thermal speed, alpha to proton density ratio, and proton-alpha flow speed difference.
