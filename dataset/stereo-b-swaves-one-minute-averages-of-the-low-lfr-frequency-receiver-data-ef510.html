Description

The two STEREO radio receivers measure wave intensity, source direction and angular size in the frequency range 40 kHz to 16 MHz, corresponding to heliocentric source distances of about 1 Rs to 1 AU. One 10-40 kHz receiver measures radio and plasma waves near the 1 AU electron plasma frequency. One receiver makes high time resolution measurements at 50 MHz. Time Domain Samplers make simultaneous wideband waveform measurements on three electric components. Antenna systems include three mutually orthogonal 6-meter monopoles.
