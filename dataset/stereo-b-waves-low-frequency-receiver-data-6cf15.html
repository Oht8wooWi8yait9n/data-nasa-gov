Description

Radio measurements of power spectral density,radio flux density, Stokes Q, U, V parameters and angular half width of source measured by the STEREO-B/WAVES instrument. There a 48 linearly spaced frequency channels from 2.61 to 153 kHz. Time resolution varies with instrument mode ranging from 15 seconds to 1 minute.
