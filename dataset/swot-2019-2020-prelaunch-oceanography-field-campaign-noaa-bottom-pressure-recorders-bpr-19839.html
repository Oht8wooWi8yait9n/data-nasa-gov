Earth ScienceScience Discovery EngineAccess: public

Description

This dataset provides the bottom pressure measurements collected during the 2019-2020 SWOT prelaunch field campaign conducted around the SWOT crossover location in the California Currents, 300km west of Monterey, California, USA. The Paroscientiﬁc Digiquartz pressure sensor was used. The data are recorded on a 15-second interval.
