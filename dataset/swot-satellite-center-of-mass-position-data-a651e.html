Description

Satellite center of mass position with respect to its reference point. The SAT_COM product provides the X/Y/Z coordinates of satellite center of mass in the KaRIn metering and structure reference frame (KMSF) with associated information of the origin of the variation. This is a historical product and the most recently available file provides a complete history since launch. Available in netCDF-4 file format with latency of < 1.5 days.
