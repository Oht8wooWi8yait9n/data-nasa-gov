Description

Cloud-Precipitation Hybrid Regimes with MODIS C6.1 2D joint histogram of CTP and COT (total 42 bins) and IMERG v06B precipitation histogram (total 6 bins) derived in 15S-15N domain. This version is for precip weight=1, so cloud:precip = 7:1 ratio. (See details in Jin et al. 2021, https://doi.org/10.1175/JAMC-D-20-0253.1) The dataset is composed of one netCDF file, which includes centroids, CPR-numbers-on-map for Terra, Aqua, and TAmean, and UTC info for Terra and Aqua observations (2002.07.16 to 2021.09.29).
