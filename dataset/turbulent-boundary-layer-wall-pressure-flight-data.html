Turbulent boundary layer wall pressure flight data

AerospaceDNG Legacy DataAccess: public
View Official Record on data.nasa.gov

Description

Wall pressure data acquired during flight tests at three flight conditions are provided: 48k feet altitude at Mach 0.86; 32k feet at Mach 0.70; and 24k feet at Mach 0.56. Details of the flight test, sensors, and sensor locations are given in a journal article that is referenced in the supporting documents datafile.
