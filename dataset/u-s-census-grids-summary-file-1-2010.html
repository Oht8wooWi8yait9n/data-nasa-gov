Description

The U.S. Census Grids (Summary File 1), 2010 data set contains grids of demographic and socioeconomic data from the year 2010 in ASCII and GeoTIFF formats. The grids have a resolution of 30 arc-seconds (0.0083 decimal degrees), or approximately 1 square km. The gridded variables are based on census block geography from Census 2010 TIGER/Line Files and census variables (population, households, and housing variables).
