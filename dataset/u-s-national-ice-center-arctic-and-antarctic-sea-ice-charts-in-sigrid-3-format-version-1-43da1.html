Earth ScienceScience Discovery EngineAccess: public
View Official Record on data.nasa.gov

Description

This data set provides total sea ice concentration, partial concentration, stage of development, and ice form as defined by the World Meteorological Organization in SIGRID-3 shapefiles. The data are from the U.S. National Ice Center (USNIC) and span 2003 to the present.
