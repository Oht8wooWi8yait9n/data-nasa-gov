Ulysses Cosmic Ray and Solar Particle Investigation (COSPIN) Kiel Electron Telescope (KET) Proton, Helium, and Electron PHA based Fluxes in ASCII Format, Daily Average Data

HeliophysicsScience Discovery EngineAccess: public
View Official Record on data.nasa.gov

Description

This Data Set contains Daily Average PHA based Fluxes of Protons (34.1-125.0, 125.0-320.0, 320.0-2100.0, >2100.0 MeV), Alpha Particles (34.2-125.0, 125.0-320.0, 320.0-2100.0, >2100.0 MeV/n), and Electrons (2.5-7.0 Mev, 2.5 GV) and the Poisson Uncertainties in those Fluxes
