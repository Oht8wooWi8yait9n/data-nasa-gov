Ulysses Heliosphere Instrument for Spectral, Composition, and Anisotropy at Low Energies (HISCALE) LEFS60 MFSA, Spacecraft Frame, Sectored Z>1 Ion Fluxes, Hourly Averages

HeliophysicsScience Discovery EngineAccess: public
View Official Record on data.nasa.gov

Description

Hourly-averaged sectored Z>1 ion fluxes from the MF Spectrum Analyzer of the Ulysses/HISCALE LEFS60 instrument. All energies thresholds take into account the incident particle type, shielding, and inactive dead-layer of the solid state detector. All fluxes are background corrected and are in the spacecraft rest frame.
