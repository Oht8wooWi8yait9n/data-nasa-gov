Ulysses Heliosphere Instrument for Spectral, Composition, and Anisotropy at Low Energies (HISCALE) LEMS120 MFSA, Solar Wind Frame, Sectored Z>1 Ion Fluxes, 17 min Averages

HeliophysicsScience Discovery EngineAccess: public
View Official Record on data.nasa.gov

Description

17-min-averaged sectored Z>1 ion fluxes from the MF Spectrum Analyzer of the Ulysses/HISCALE LEMS120 instrument. All energies thresholds take into account the incident particle type, shielding, and inactive dead-layer of the solid state detector. All fluxes are background corrected and are in the solar wind rest frame.
