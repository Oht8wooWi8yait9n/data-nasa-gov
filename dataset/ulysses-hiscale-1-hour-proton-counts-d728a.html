Ulysses HISCALE 1-hour proton counts

HeliophysicsScience Discovery EngineAccess: public
View Official Record on data.nasa.gov

Description

This data set contains 1-hour spin averaged count rates of proton data from the Ulysses Heliosphere Instrument for Spectra, Composition and Anisotropy at Low Energies (HI-SCALE) Composition Aperture Telescope at 60 degrees to the spacecraft spin axis. Count rate units are /s. For references, see Astron. Astrophys. Suppl. Ser., 92(2), 349-363, 1992.
