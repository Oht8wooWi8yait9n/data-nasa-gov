ULYSSES JUPITER HISCALE LEFS 60 ELECTRON/ION COUNTS

Planetary ScienceScience Discovery EngineAccess: public

Description

This data set consists of HISCALE Low-Energy Foil Spectrometer (LEFS) electron and ion counts at 60 degrees from the spacecraft spin axis. These measurements were taken during the Ulysses Jupiter encounter 1991-12-31 to 1992-02-16, and include 1 hour averaged inbound cruise data (1991-12-31 to 1992-02-01), and 15 minute averaged encounter data (1992-02-02 to 1992-02-16).
