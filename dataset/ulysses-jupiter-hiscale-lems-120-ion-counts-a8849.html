ULYSSES JUPITER HISCALE LEMS 120 ION COUNTS

Earth ScienceDNG Legacy DataAccess: public
View Official Record on data.nasa.gov

Description

This data set consists of HISCALE Low-Energy Magnetic Spectrometer (LEFS) ion counts at 120 degrees from the spacecraft spin axis. These measurements were taken during the Ulysses Jupiter encounter 1991-12-31 to 1992-02-16, and include 1 hour averaged inbound cruise data (1991-12-31 to 1992-02-01), and 15 minute averaged encounter data (1992-02-02 to 1992-02-16).
