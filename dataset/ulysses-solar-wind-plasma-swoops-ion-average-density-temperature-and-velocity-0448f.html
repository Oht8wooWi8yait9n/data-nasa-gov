Ulysses Solar Wind Plasma (SWOOPS) Ion Average Density, Temperature and Velocity

HeliophysicsScience Discovery EngineAccess: public

Description

This Data Set contains 1 hr Averages of the Ion Density, Temperature and Velocity Data from the Ulysses Solar Wind Observations Over the Poles of the Sun, SWOOPS, Instrument. Density Units are cm^-3, Temperature Units are °K, Velocity Units are km/s. Data Set Contact: B.E. Goldstein, NASA Ames Research Center, USA. Principal Investigator: D.J. McComas, Southwest Research Institute, USA. Reference: Astron. Astrophys. Suppl. Ser., 92(2), 237-265, 1992. Relevant Web Sites: http://swoops.lanl.gov/
