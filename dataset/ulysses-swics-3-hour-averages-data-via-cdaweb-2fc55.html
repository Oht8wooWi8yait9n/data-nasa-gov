Description

This data set contains 3-hour averages of ion data from the Ulysses Solar Wind Ion Composition Spectrometer (SWICS) instrument. Density, velocity, and temperature data are available for 10 speciess (H, He, C, N, O, Ne, Mg, Si, S, and Fe) and charge states at 3-hr resolution.
