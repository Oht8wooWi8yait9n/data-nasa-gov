HeliophysicsScience Discovery EngineAccess: public
View Official Record on data.nasa.gov

Description

This data set contains hourly averages of the ion density for protons and alpha particles, temperatures (min and max), and velocity data in RTN components from the Ulysses Solar Wind Observations Over the Poles of the Sun (SWOOPS) instrument. Density units are /cm3, temperature units are K, velocity units are km/s.
