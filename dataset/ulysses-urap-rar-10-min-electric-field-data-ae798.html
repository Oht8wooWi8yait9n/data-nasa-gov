HeliophysicsScience Discovery EngineAccess: public

Description

This set of RAR (Radio Astronomy Receiver) data from the Ulysses URAP (Unified Radio and Plasma Waves) experiment contains averaged value frequency spectra for electric field intensities in 10-minute intervals via CDAWeb. The frequency spectra are determined from measurements on the X antenna of the URAP instrument in units of microvolt/Hz^0.5 for the frequency channel from 1.25 - 940.0 kHz.
