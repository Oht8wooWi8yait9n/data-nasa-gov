Van Allen Probe A Electric and Magnetic Field Instrument Suite and Integrated Science (EMFISIS) Waveform Receiver (WFR) Cross Spectral Matrix, Level 2 (L2), 6 s Data

HeliophysicsScience Discovery EngineAccess: public
View Official Record on data.nasa.gov

Description

Three Axis Electric Field (EU, EV, EW) and Three Axis Magnetic field (BU, BV, BW) cross spectral matrix, 6 diagonal components and 15 off-diagonal components. The instrument coordinates U, V (spin plane antenna) and W (spin axis antenna) are in the spinning spacecraft frame. Frequency range: 2 Hz to 11kHz, Frequency Bins: 65 quasi-logarithmic spaced, cadence: 1 spectra per 6 seconds (survey).
