Description

High Frequency Receiver (HFR) data of Single Axis AC Electric Field Spectra (Selectable between the U, V and W Axis). Frequency range: 10 kHz to 486.97kHz (Band width:480 Hz to 23.231kHz). 82 logarithmically spaced frequency bins. Cadence: 1 spectra per 6 seconds.
