Van Allen Probe B High Resolution DC Electric Field in UVW Coordinates

HeliophysicsScience Discovery EngineAccess: public
View Official Record on data.nasa.gov

Description

This data set contains high-resolution DC Electric Field data in UVW coordinates as measured by the Electric Field and Waves Suite (EFW) onboard Van Allen Probe B. As a reference for spin axis vector in GSE, there was no spinning-frame offset removal.
