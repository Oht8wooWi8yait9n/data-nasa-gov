Earth ScienceDNG Legacy DataAccess: public

Description

The longest-lived in-situ measurement platforms at Venus have been the Soviet VEGA balloons in 1985 and the only high-quality pressure/temperature profile in the lowest 10km of the atmosphere is that from the VEGA-2 lander.
