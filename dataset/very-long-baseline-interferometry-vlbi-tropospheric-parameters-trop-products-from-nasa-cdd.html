Earth ScienceScience Discovery EngineAccess: public

Description

Troposphere Zenith Path Delay (ZPD) values derived from analysis of Very Long Baseline Interferometry (VLBI) data. These products are the generated by analysis centers in support of the International VLBI Service for Geodesy and Astrometry (IVS).
