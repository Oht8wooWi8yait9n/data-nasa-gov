VG1 J PLASMA WAVE SPECTROMETER DENSITY 1S V1.0

Planetary ScienceScience Discovery EngineAccess: public
View Official Record on data.nasa.gov

Description

Electron plasma density data set for Jupiter's outer magnetosphere derived from high-resolution wideband measurements of low-frequency radio and plasma waves obtained by Voyagers 1 and 2 during their 1979 flybys. Density data were measured at 1-second intervals.
