Description

This data set describes data of the counting rate from the D1 detector in the Cosmic Ray System (CRS) electron telescope (TET) on Voyager 2 during the Uranus encounter. The D1 detector nominally responds to electrons with kinetic energies above approximately 1 MeV (see detector description for details). Note that the instrument is saturated near the maximum of counting rate of approximately 50,000 counts/sec between approximately 1986-01-24T18:10:50Z and 1986-01-24T19:02:02Z. When they are near saturation, the counting rates should be corrected for deadtime according to the formula in the DATA_SET_OR_INST_PARM_DESC: {corrected rate} = {uncorrected rate/(1+deadtime*{uncorrected rate}).
