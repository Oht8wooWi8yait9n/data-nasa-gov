Description

This data set includes data from the Low Field Magnetometer (LFM) in the solar wind the near the Uranus encounter. The actual encounter data (1986-01-24T07:00:00 -> 1986-01-25T04:00:00) are provided in the Uranus Longitude coordinate system. The magnetometer data in the solar wind are given in Heliographic coordinates and the data have been averaged from the 1.92 second resampled data to a 9.6 second resampled rate.
