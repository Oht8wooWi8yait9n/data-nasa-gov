Description

The Near Real Time (NRT) JPSS1/VIIRS Raw Radiances in Counts 6-Min L1A Swath (VJ101_NRT) data product contains the unpacked, raw VIIRS science, calibration and engineering data; the extracted ephemeris and attitude data from the spacecraft diary packets; and the raw ADCS and bus-critical spacecraft telemetry data from those packets, with a few critical fields extracted. For more information download VIIRS Level 1 Product [User's Guide](https://oceancolor.gsfc.nasa.gov/docs/format/VIIRS_Level-1_DataProductUsersGuide.pdf)
