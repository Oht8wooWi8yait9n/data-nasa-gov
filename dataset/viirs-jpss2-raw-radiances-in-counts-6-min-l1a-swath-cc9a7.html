Description

The Near Real Time (NRT) VIIRS/JPSS2 Raw Radiances in Counts 6-Min L1A Swath, short-name VJ201_NRT data product contains the unpacked, raw VIIRS science, calibration and engineering data; the extracted ephemeris and attitude data from the spacecraft diary packets; and the raw ADCS and bus-critical spacecraft telemetry data from those packets, with a few critical fields extracted.
