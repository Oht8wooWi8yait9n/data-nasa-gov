Description

This data set contains 85 published spectra of asteroids obtained by Faith Vilas during the years 1982 - 1992. These appeared in the following papers: [VILASETAL1985]; [VILAS&SMITH1985]; [VILAS&MCFADDEN1992]; [VILASETAL1993A]; [VILASETAL1993B]
