Wind 3DP Electron Core Halo Strahl Moments, 96s, year files

HeliophysicsScience Discovery EngineAccess: public
View Official Record on data.nasa.gov

Description

These ~96s electron core halo and strahl parameters are moments of Wind 3DP EESA-L and EESA-H measurements, corrected for spacecraft potential and other instrumental effects.
