Wind Magnetic Field Investigation (MFI) 15-sec magnetic field data, solar wind only

HeliophysicsScience Discovery EngineAccess: public
View Official Record on data.nasa.gov

Description

Data consist of 15-sec averages of magnetic field magnitude and GSE Cartesian components, from the MFI magnetometer on Wind. Data are for only the solar wind phases of the Wind orbit.
